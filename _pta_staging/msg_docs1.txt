docs: land the PTA integration designs and the analyses they came from

Two design documents that have existed only as working files until now, plus
the two source analyses they were written against.

pta_cpu_integration.md argues that the c930 NPU is already the right host for a
photonic tensor tile -- its weight-stationary systolic dataflow is the dataflow
a photonic tile wants -- and works out what changes: the precision codes and CSR
space, the PTM-C compatibility shim against the PTM-B broadside variant, an
analog error model that keeps a bitwise numerical gate rather than abandoning
one, and calibration scheduled in the DMA's memory shadow.

pta_gpu_integration.md takes the opposite result seriously. The FEDP backend
seam in grxgpu is real -- five backends, each declaring its own FEDP_LATENCY --
but it is the wrong seam for a photonic engine, because nothing is stationary
there and FEDP_K = 2. It is the right seam for an analog-numerics study. The
engine itself belongs at cluster scope beside VX_dxa_core.

Both are marked design-not-built, and the GPU path is staged behind the CPU
path's phase C2 gate.

The source analyses are committed alongside because the designs cite them by
path and a clone without them cannot check the citations.
